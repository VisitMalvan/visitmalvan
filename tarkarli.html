Why Visit Tarkarli?

Tarkarli is one of Maharashtra's most spectacular coastal destinations, famous for its crystal-clear Arabian Sea, powdery white sand beaches and exciting adventure activities.

Whether you're planning a romantic getaway, a family vacation or an adventure trip with friends, Tarkarli promises unforgettable memories with its natural beauty, marine life, delicious Malvani cuisine and warm local hospitality.
