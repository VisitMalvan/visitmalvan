DISCOVER THE FORT

A Historic Sea Fort In The Arabian Sea

Sindhudurg Fort is one of Maharashtra's most famous historical monuments, built by Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj in 1664.

Situated in the Arabian Sea near Malvan, the fort offers breathtaking sea views, historical significance and an unforgettable sightseeing experience.
