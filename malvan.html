WHY VISIT

Why Visit Malvan?

Malvan is one of Maharashtra's most beautiful coastal destinations, known for its crystal-clear Arabian Sea waters, pristine beaches, historic Sindhudurg Fort and authentic Malvani culture.

Whether you are looking for adventure, relaxation, local food, sightseeing or a peaceful beach holiday, Malvan offers something for every traveller.

GETTING HERE

How To Reach Malvan

Malvan is well connected by road, rail and air.

🚗

By Road

Malvan can be easily reached by road from Goa, Mumbai, Pune and nearby cities.

🚆

By Train

Kudal is the nearest major railway station, with road transport available to Malvan.

✈️

By Air

Goa and Sindhudurg airports can be used depending on your travel route.
