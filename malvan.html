Why Visit Malvan?

Nestled along Maharashtra's picturesque Konkan coastline, Malvan is famous for its crystal-clear beaches, historic Sindhudurg Fort, thrilling water sports and delicious Malvani seafood.

Whether you're planning a romantic getaway, family vacation or adventure holiday, Malvan offers the perfect blend of relaxation, history and unforgettable coastal experiences.

How To Reach Malvan

Malvan is well connected by road, rail and air.

By Road

Well connected via Mumbai, Pune, Kolhapur and Goa through National Highways.

By Train

Nearest railway stations: Kudal (30 km), Sindhudurg (35 km), Sawantwadi (45 km).

By Air

Nearest Airports: Goa (Dabolim) Mopa International Airport Both offer convenient road access.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before planning your Malvan holiday.

How many days are enough for Malvan?

A 2 to 3-day trip is ideal to enjoy beaches, Sindhudurg Fort, water sports and nearby attractions.

What is the best time to visit Malvan?

October to March offers pleasant weather and is perfect for sightseeing and beach activities.

Is scuba diving safe in Malvan?

Yes. Certified instructors provide safety equipment and guidance for beginners and experienced divers.

Can families visit Malvan?

Absolutely. Malvan is one of Maharashtra's most family-friendly beach destinations with activities for all age groups.

How far is Tarkarli from Malvan?

Tarkarli is approximately 7 km from Malvan and can be reached within 15 minutes by road.
