WHY VISIT

Why Visit Tarkarli?

Tarkarli is Maharashtra's most celebrated beach destination, renowned for its crystal-clear Arabian Sea waters, powdery white sand, thrilling water sports and unforgettable marine adventures.

Located just 7 km from Malvan, Tarkarli is home to India's finest scuba diving experiences, exciting dolphin safaris, beautiful backwaters, houseboats and peaceful sunsets. Whether you're planning a romantic getaway, family vacation or adventure trip, Tarkarli has something for everyone.
